Privacy Policy, Terms of Service & Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 1, 2026 | Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Zero-Knowledge Security Architecture Principles

HiddenData ('we', 'us', or 'the Software') is an independent zero-knowledge encrypted media vault studio engineered to guarantee absolute data privacy.

The Software employs AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation. Your master password, encryption keys, and raw media files are processed exclusively inside your local device's volatile memory (RAM) and are never transmitted to or stored on remote servers. As a client-side zero-knowledge architecture, no backdoor keys exist, ensuring only the master credential holder can decrypt the data.

1.2 Google Drive API Usage & Compliance with Limited Use Requirements

HiddenData utilizes the Google Drive REST API solely for cloud vault backup and cross-platform synchronization between the PC Chrome Extension and the Android Mobile Application.

We strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Principle of Least Privilege: We request only the 'https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file' scope. This scope is strictly restricted to encrypted files (.enc), the master configuration file (.hiddendata_vault_config.json), and the license sync file (.hiddendata_pro_license.json) created directly by HiddenData.
  • No Access to Other Files: The Software has no permission, access, or ability to view or modify any of your other documents, photos, or folders stored in your Google Drive.
  • No Third-Party Transfer or AI Training: Google user data is never sold, transferred to third-party brokers or advertisers, nor used to train artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

1.3 Chrome Extension Permissions Specification

The PC Chrome extension requests only the essential permissions required for its vault operations:

  • sidePanel: Provides the side panel interface to inspect and manage your vault while browsing.
  • storage: Safely persists local preferences and the Zero-Knowledge Verifier hash inside your local browser to reject incorrect passwords instantly.
  • identity: Acquires secure OAuth2 tokens upon your explicit request to connect Google Drive and sync your Pro license.
  • activeTab: Facilitates secure drag-and-drop ingestion of web images or files into your encrypted vault.

1.4 Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Because we never store user data or master passwords on remote servers, data deletion is entirely under your control. Deleting your local vault folder or removing the HiddenData_Vault folder from Google Drive completely and permanently deletes your data.
