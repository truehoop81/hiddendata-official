02.

Comprehensive Technical FAQ

Detailed technical explanations answering critical questions regarding zero-disk streaming, cryptographic keys, performance, offline capabilities, and cloud safety.

Q1. How does Zero-Disk in-memory streaming guarantee no forensic trace remains on my computer?
Traditional file encryption vaults create temporary decrypted files in system folders (such as %TEMP% or /tmp) while media is open. When closed, OS deletion only unlinks the file pointer, leaving unallocated disk clusters intact. Digital forensics software (EnCase, FTK Imager, PhotoRec) can readily reconstruct these unallocated clusters. In stark contrast, HiddenData decrypts files strictly within volatile RAM typed arrays in 64KB AEAD chunks. Plaintext data never touches physical storage (SSD, HDD, or flash). Upon closing the viewer or switching tabs, all RAM buffers are immediately overwritten with zeros (Arrays.fill / buffer wipe). As a result, digital forensic extraction is technically impossible.
Q2. Why is master password recovery technically impossible, even by HiddenData engineers?
HiddenData is designed on a pure Zero-Knowledge foundation. Key derivation occurs locally on your hardware using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and a cryptographically secure 16-byte random salt. Neither your master password nor the derived encryption key is ever transmitted over the network or saved to any central database. We intentionally do not build recovery mechanisms, security questions, or administrative escrow keys because any backdoor created for recovery would inherently become a vulnerability for state actors or malicious hackers to exploit. Please store your master password securely.
Q3. Can I play high-bitrate 4K 60fps video and large animated GIFs smoothly without lag?
Yes. HiddenData partitions encrypted payloads into 64KB independent AEAD chunks. When you play a 5GB 4K video, the engine does not wait to decrypt the full 5GB; it decrypts only the specific 64KB chunks required by the player's immediate playback head. Seeking to minute 45:00 executes an instant random-access read, authenticating and decoding the target chunks in under 15 milliseconds. Hardware-accelerated AES-NI and Web Crypto primitives ensure buttery-smooth 60fps video and seamless GIF loops without battery drain.
Q4. Does HiddenData work completely offline without an active internet connection?
Yes, completely. All core cryptographic features (encrypting files, decrypting galleries, streaming video in RAM, rotating passwords via KeySlotManager) execute entirely offline inside your local device processor. Internet access is exclusively needed if you choose to connect Google Drive for cloud backup or sync across devices.
Q5. How do the Android app and PC Chrome Extension share the exact same encrypted vault?
Both Android and the Chrome Extension are built around the identical V3 Cryptographic Envelope binary standard. When you encrypt a photo on your Android device and save it to your Google Drive cloud vault (or an external USB-C SSD), the exact same binary .enc container is readable by the PC Chrome Extension Side Panel using the same master password. No file conversion or re-encryption is ever necessary.
Q6. What happens if Google Drive disconnects or goes offline during backup?
HiddenData employs transactional, chunked resumable upload and download sessions. If your Wi-Fi drops or your phone enters battery saver mode mid-transfer, HiddenData tracks chunk progress and resumes cleanly once connectivity is restored. Incomplete uploads never overwrite existing verified encrypted files.
Q7. Why did HiddenData choose the drive.file permission scope instead of full Google Drive access?
Requesting the full https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive permission scope gives an app unrestricted access to inspect, modify, and delete every spreadsheet, private document, and personal photo in your Google Drive. HiddenData strictly uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, granting access only to files created by HiddenData itself. Our code cannot even query or detect the existence of your other Google Drive files.

Q9. What media and document formats are supported by HiddenData Studio?
HiddenData Studio supports all modern visual and audio formats: PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, animated GIF, SVG, BMP, HEIC/HEIF, MP4 (H.264 / AV1 / HEVC), WebM (VP9), MKV, MOV, MP3, WAV, and FLAC. In addition, any arbitrary confidential file (PDF, ZIP, TXT) can be stored as an encrypted binary container.
Q10. How does the One-Touch Stealth Mode protect against shoulder surfing in public?
When viewing sensitive media in public (e.g. on a train, in an open-plan office, or at a café), someone might unexpectedly look over your shoulder. Pressing the quick panic key (or tapping the eye shield icon) immediately blacks out/blurs the viewing canvas and freezes in-memory decoders in under 5 milliseconds. The stage remains obscured until you safely re-confirm your presence.
