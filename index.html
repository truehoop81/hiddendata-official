FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What happens if I forget my master password?

HiddenData is engineered on a strict Zero-Knowledge architecture: your passwords and encryption keys never leave your device. Because no backdoors exist, nobody—including developers—can ever restore your password. Please store it safely.

Q. Is this a subscription?

No. HiddenData Pro is a one-time purchase (Lifetime License). You will never be charged recurring subscription fees.

Q. Can I use the license on both PC and mobile?

Yes! Through Google Drive synchronization, your Pro license is automatically recognized across both the PC Chrome extension and the Android mobile app (early-access & official releases).

Q. Does HiddenData access my other files in Google Drive?

Never. HiddenData strictly requests the limited drive.file OAuth scope, which only grants access to files specifically created by HiddenData (.enc files and config files). We have zero access to your other Google Drive files.
